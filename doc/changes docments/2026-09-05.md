## Purchase Order Dynamic Clear & Quantity Reduction Architecture

- **Purchase Order Store Dynamic Clear (`usePurchaseOrderStore.ts`)**:
  - Implemented `clearActiveTarget()` method targeting the selected item and active numpad mode:
    - **Cost Rate Mode (`cost`)**: Resets the active item's purchasing cost price (`costPrice`) to `0.00`.
    - **Selling Rate Mode (`price`)**: Resets the active item's retail selling price (`newPrice`) to `0.00`.
    - **Quantity Mode (`qty`)**: Implemented standard POS 0-step quantity reduction logic:
      - If `item.quantity > 0`: reduces quantity via `Math.floor(item.quantity / 10)` (e.g. `25` -> `2`, single digits like `1` or `7` become `0` first and remain in the cart).
      - If `item.quantity === 0`: removes the item from the cart on the subsequent clear press.
  - Updated `handleNumpadInput` in `usePurchaseOrderStore.ts` for backspace (`backspace`) to match this exact 0-step reduction behavior.

- **Numpad Toolbar Clear Button Separation (`PurchaseOrderNumpad.vue`)**:
  - Separated item-level clearing from whole-cart clearing:
    - Replaced `poStore.clearCart` on the numpad toolbar button (button 4) with `poStore.clearActiveTarget()`.
    - Added an eraser icon (`fa-eraser`) and dynamic contextual tooltip (`clearTooltip`) explaining whether it reduces/removes quantity or resets cost/sell rate to 0.00.
  - Retained the top-level **Clear Cart** button in the cart lines list header of `InventoryHistoryView.vue` for clearing all items from the PO cart.

- **0-Quantity Visual Cues & Safe Submission (`PurchaseOrderCartItem.vue`, `InventoryHistoryView.vue`)**:
  - Added visual cues in `PurchaseOrderCartItem.vue` for items at 0 quantity:
    - Line-through text styling on product title (`line-through text-gray-400`).
    - Alert pill highlighting on Quantity box (`bg-rose-50 text-rose-700 border-rose-200 ring-1 ring-rose-300 animate-pulse` or active rose ring).
    - Status badge labeled `0 Qty` indicating that the next press of Clear or Backspace will remove the item.
    - Guarded margin calculations against division by 0 when quantity or selling price is 0.
  - Updated `requestSubmitBatch` and `executeSubmitBatch` in `InventoryHistoryView.vue` to filter out lines with `quantity <= 0` and display a warning toast if no items with quantity > 0 exist.

## Decoupling Vendor Payments from Inventory Restocking & Dedicated Accounts Payable Tab

- **Separation of Concerns (Accounts Payable Architecture)**:
  - Decoupled physical inventory receiving from vendor payments:
    - Restocking inventory through Purchase Orders records the received stock into inventory (`+Qty`) and logs the purchase cost to the vendor's ledger as an **unpaid credit payable liability** (`vendor.balance += totalCost`).
    - Removed the automatic popup of `VendorPaymentModal` upon PO creation in `InventoryHistoryView.vue`. POs are now confirmed cleanly without forcing immediate payment.
- **Dedicated Vendors & Accounts Payable Hub Tab (`InventoryHistoryView.vue`)**:

    - **Debit Note Claims**: Total value and count of damaged/expired items in hold queue pending vendor return credit.
  - **Supplier Ledger & Balances Table**:
    - Complete directory of suppliers with contact details, company names, and real-time outstanding balances.
    - Color-coded balance badges: `Payable: Rs X.XX` (red), `Settled (Rs 0.00)` (gray), and `Advance: Rs X.XX` (green).
    - Quick actions: **`Pay`** (opens payment modal pre-filled with supplier's balance) and **`Order`** (starts a PO with that supplier pre-selected).
  - **Past Vendor Payments & Settlements Audit Ledger**:
    - Audit log of past settlements showing date, supplier name, payment method (Cash, Bank Transfer, Cheque, Debit Note Credit), amount paid, debit note deductions, cashier, and invoice/notes.

## Session End Report at Session Closing

- **Financial Aggregation & Session Report Engine (`src/stores/useSessionStore.ts`)**:
  - Defined `SessionEndReportData` interface capturing the complete closing audit structure:
    - `1) Opening Cash`: Base float loaded into drawer at register launch (`openingCash`).
    - `2) Total Sales`:
      - Gross Sales & Net Sales totals and transaction counts.
      - `i) Cash Sales`: Orders tendered in Cash (`cashSalesTotal`, `cashSalesCount`).
      - `ii) Credit Sales`: Orders billed to Customer Account / Credit (`creditSalesTotal`, `creditSalesCount`).
      - `iii) Card Sales`: Orders processed via POS card swipe / bank transfer (`cardSalesTotal`, `cardSalesCount`).
      - `iv) Sales Returns`: Customer returns and refunds during the session in negative amount (`salesReturnsTotal`, `salesReturnsCount`).
    - `3) Payments`:
      - `i) Vendors Payments`: Cash paid out of register drawer to suppliers (`vendorPaymentsTotal`, `vendorPaymentsCount`).
      - Register safe drops / petty cash expenses (`cashDropsTotal`, `cashDropsCount`).
    - `4) Expected Closing Cash That Should Be There`:
      - Exact drawer reconciliation formula:
        `openingCash + cashSalesTotal - cashSalesReturns - vendorPaymentsTotal - cashDropsTotal`.
    - `5) Reconciliation`: Counted cash input and live difference/variance tracking (`cashVariance`).
  - Added `loadSessionReport(targetSessionId?: string)` action querying `schema.sales`, `schema.sales_returns`, `schema.vendor_payments`, and `schema.cash_drops` for real-time audit data.
  - Updated `closeSession(countedCash, reportData)` to persist `expected_closing_balance`, `closing_cash_counted`, and `cash_variance` into `schema.cash_sessions`.

- **Customer Credit Payment Method in Checkout (`src/components/modals/CheckoutModal.vue`)**:
  - Added **Credit** (Customer Account / Pay on Account) payment method alongside **Cash** and **Bank / Card**.
  - Configured 3-button tender grid with purple styling (`border-[#714B67] bg-purple-50 text-[#714B67]`) to easily record Credit Sales.

- **Executive Session End Report & Closing Cash Control Modal (`src/components/modals/ClosingCashModal.vue`)**:

    - **Revenue Breakdown Table**: Itemized rows with colored badges and counts for Cash Sales (emerald), Credit Sales (purple), Card Sales (blue), and Sales Returns (rose, negative `-Rs XX.XX`).
