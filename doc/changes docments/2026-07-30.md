  - Resolved the issue where page reloads caused newly created products to disappear.

    - Unit of Measure selector (`Pcs`, `Grams`, `Liters`, `Kg`).

  - Updated `pos_backend/src/routes/products.ts` to store BOM recipe lines (`product_bom` & `product_bom_lines`) for automatic raw inventory deduction on POS sales!
