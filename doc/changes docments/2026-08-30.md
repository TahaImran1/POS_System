## POS Cart Quantity Reduction & Discount Fixes

- **Item Quantity Reduction 0-Step Behavior (`useCartStore.ts`)**:
  - Modified `handleNumpadInput` in `qty` mode so reducing a single-digit quantity (e.g. `1` or `7`) reduces `item.quantity` to `0` first (retaining the item in the cart).
  - Pressing remove/backspace a second time when `quantity === 0` removes the item from the cart ticket.
  - Added visual styling in `CartItem.vue` highlighting 0-quantity lines with line-through and a red pill badge.
  - Added a checkout safeguard filtering out 0-quantity items when processing sales.

- **Discount 100% Capping (`useCartStore.ts` & `CartItem.vue`)**:
  - Enforced a hard max cap of `100%` discount in `handleNumpadInput` in `disc` mode, `subtotal` calculation getter, and item row rendering.
  - Prevented discounts from exceeding 100% or resulting in negative line items.

## Sales Return & Exchange Module

- **Original Sale Immutability Architecture**:
  - Created `sales_returns` and `sales_return_items` SQLite tables to store standalone return records linked to `origin_sale_id`. Original sale records in `sales` are **never altered or modified**.
- **POS-Style Pre-Loaded Return Register (`SaleReturnModal.vue`)**:
  - Pre-loads original items from searched sales order into the POS cart register at their **Original Purchase Rates (OLD)**.
- **Dynamic Dual Pricing Engine (`bomService.ts` & `SaleReturnModal.vue`)**:
  - **Returned Quantities**: Refunded at the **OLD price** at the time of sale.
  - **Exchange Items / Added Quantities**: Allows cashier to toggle between **NEW Rate** (current store price) and **OLD Rate** (original purchase price, ideal when exchanging damaged/expired items without penalizing customers).
  - Automatically calculates Net Settlement (Net Cash Refund to Customer vs Net Balance Payable).
- **Condition-Based Inventory Auditing (`bomService.ts`)**:
  - `RESTOCKABLE` (Good Condition): Restocks item (+qty) into inventory.
  - `DAMAGED` / `EXPIRED`: Logs return (+qty `CUSTOMER_RETURN`) and immediate loss deduction (-qty `DAMAGE_LOSS` / `EXPIRY_LOSS`), maintaining a 100% traceable audit trail with 0 net stock inflation.
- **Granular Rights Verification (`useAuthStore.ts` & `Numpad.vue`)**:
  - Uses `authStore.hasRight('void_orders')` permission check. Any user granted the `void_orders` right can perform returns; non-authorized staff require Manager PIN.

## Vendor Management, PO Carton Units & Damaged/Expired Hold Queue Debit Notes

- **Database Schemas (`schema.ts` & `schema.sql.ts`)**:
  - Created `vendors` table for supplier records (`vendor_id`, `name`, `company_name`, `phone`, `email`, `address`, `balance`).
  - Created `damaged_expired_hold` table for logging returned damaged/expired items awaiting vendor debit note claims (`hold_id`, `product_id`, `quantity`, `cost_price`, `condition`, `status`).
  - Created `vendor_payments` table for recording payments and debit note credits (`payment_id`, `vendor_id`, `po_id`, `session_id`, `amount`, `debit_note_amount`, `payment_method`).
- **Vendor Service (`vendorService.ts`)**:
  - Implemented `getVendors()`, `createVendor()`, `getDamagedExpiredHoldQueue()`, and `processVendorPaymentWithDebitNote()`.
  - Automatically logs cash payouts as `CASH_OUT_VENDOR_PAYMENT` in active shift session when paid via Cash.
- **Purchase Order (PO) Carton Units & Cost Rates (`InventoryHistoryView.vue`)**:
  - Added unit mode toggle (`Pcs` vs `Carton / Pack`), Pack Size (units/carton), and Carton Cost Price.
  - Automatically calculates total piece stock additions (`Cartons × Pack Size`) and purchasing cost valuation.
  - Separates **Purchasing Cost Rate** (for PO Bill & Vendor Payable) from **Selling Retail Rate** (for POS sales).
- **Damaged / Expired Hold Queue & Sales Returns (`bomService.ts` & `SaleReturnModal.vue`)**:
  - Customer sales returns for Damaged/Expired items are logged directly to `damaged_expired_hold` queue with status `PENDING_CLAIM`. Sellable inventory stock is untouched.
- **Vendor Payment & Debit Note Modal (`VendorPaymentModal.vue`, `Numpad.vue` & `ActionsModal.vue`)**:
  - Created interactive modal allowing direct vendor payments and Debit Note claims against held damaged/expired items.
  - Selected hold items are marked as `VENDOR_RECLAIMED` and their claim total (`Qty × Cost Rate`) is credited against vendor balances.
  - Automatically prompts for Vendor Payment immediately after a Purchase Order is submitted.
  - Integrated "Pay Vendor" quick action into POS Numpad and More Actions menu.

## Per-Product Nested Unit of Measure (UOM) System

- **Database Schemas (`schema.ts` & `schema.sql.ts`)**:
  - Added `product_uom` table for defining custom hierarchy multipliers (`uom_id`, `product_id`, `uom_name`, `multiplier_to_base`, `cost_price`, `selling_price`, `is_base_uom`).
  - Added `product_barcodes` table for linking physical barcodes to specific UOM tiers (`barcode_id`, `barcode`, `product_id`, `uom_id`).
- **UOM Service (`uomService.ts`)**:
  - Implemented `getProductUOMs()`, `saveProductUOMs()` (with acyclic positive multiplier validation `multiplier > 0`), and `resolveBarcode()` for physical barcode scanner lookup.
- **Base Unit Inventory Ledger (`bomService.ts` & `useCartStore.ts`)**:
  - All inventory stock additions (POs) and deductions (POS Sales) are strictly processed and logged in **Base Units** (`base_qty = qty * multiplier_to_base`).
- **Upper Hierarchy POS Selector (`CartItem.vue`)**:
  - Added interactive **UOM Hierarchy Selector** badge & dropdown to cart line items.
  - Cashiers can switch any sale line between upper hierarchy tiers (`Piece`, `Pack`, `Box`, `Carton`).
  - Switching tiers updates unit price to tier selling rate, displays UOM badge, and deducts correct base units upon checkout.
- **UOM Hierarchy Builder (`CreateProductModal.vue`)**:
  - Added **Nested Units of Measure (UOM) Hierarchy Builder Card** in product management modal allowing custom UOM tiers per product.
