- **Dynamic Real-Time Session Engine Integration**:
  - Replaced static placeholder numbers on the POS Dashboard card with **real-time computed properties** matching Odoo's backend `pos.session` architecture:
    - **Opening Balance**: Dynamically reads cashier's opening float balance.
    - **Sold**: Dynamically sums completed sales records from SQLite database (`sales` table) and counts total paid orders.
    - **Ongoing**: Dynamically sums items and taxes across all open active tickets/tables in `useCartStore` and counts total ongoing orders in real-time!
    - **Closing Balance & Date**: Dynamically formats date and closing cash count from the latest closed cash session.
